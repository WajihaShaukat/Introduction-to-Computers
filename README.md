##Introduction to Computers
A computer is an electronic device that accepts raw data as input, processes it using instructions, stores the results, and outputs meaningful information. Main components of computer are hardware, and software. Computer works on four steps: 
INPUT: Entering raw data or commands through devices like a keyboard or mouse.
PROCESSING: The central processing unit calculates and transforms tha data.
STORAGE: Saving data temporarily in RAM or permanently on a hard device.
OUTPUT: Presenting the final result on a monitor, printer, or speaker.

##Types of Computers

Computers are classified into different types based on their size, power, and how they handle data.

*Supercomputers:*
The largest and fastest computers. They process massive ammounts of data for complex tasks like weather forecasting and scientific research.

*Mainframe Computer:*
Large, powerful systems used by big organizations. They handle high-volume data processing, secure banking transactions, and enterprise operations.

*Minicomputers:*
Medium sized computers that sit between mainframes and microcomputers. They support medium-scale business operations and industrial automation.

*Microcomputers:*
Small, affordable computers designed for single users. This category includes desktops, laptops, tablets, and microphones.

##History of Computers

The history of computers spans from ancient mechanical counting tools to modern artificial intelligence and quantum processors.
ABACUS: The earliest tool used for counting and simple math.
PASCALINE: Invented by Blaise Pascal to help with tax collection, it was the first mechanical digital calculator that used dials for addition.
ANALYTICAL ENGINE: Designed by Charles Babbage, a mechanical computer concept using punched cards that laid the groundwork for modern programmable architecture.
